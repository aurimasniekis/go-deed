# Acceptance matrix

Maps every required capability from the product specification to its implementation, tests and documentation.
Status: **done** (implemented and tested locally), **partial** (implemented; the listed part is missing or misses
its target), **external** (implemented and tested against fixtures or local stand-ins; validation against the real
service, or publication, needs an owner action or an unavailable service). Nothing is removed to make completion
easier. "e2e §18" refers to `scripts/e2e.sh`, recorded in `docs/VALIDATION.md`; "Playwright" to `web/e2e/`.
